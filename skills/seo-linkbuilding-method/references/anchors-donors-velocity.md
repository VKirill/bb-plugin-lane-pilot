Source: «SEO Монстр 2027» ch6 pp. 475–476, 480–487, 497–502, 520, 538–539.

## Context rules (pp. 475, 484–486)

- 200–300-character passage around each link (250–300 on p. 475) containing the main entity + 2–3 salient QBST terms.
- Neutral pointers always need QBST-dense surrounding text.
- Specialist profiles: compound anchor = role + organization + name.
- Every partial-match anchor to the same page gets its own syntax and modifiers — no clones across dozens of domains.
- Citation/asset links: brand, URL, study, tool or expert name, "full report", "methodology", "source data".
- Paid placements: publisher sets rel attributes and may edit the anchor.
- Stop trigger: commercial exact-match ≈70% of an acceptor's profile → stop commercial anchors immediately; next waves branded/URL/informational-problem wording (doctor names, equipment models, addresses, preparation rules).
- Anti-patterns: transactional anchors ("buy", "price", "cheap"); bare surname anchors; a fixed branded/commercial ratio used as cover for a manipulative scheme.

## Donor types → acceptor map (pp. 480–483)

| Donor type | Targets |
|---|---|
| Editorial media | L1, L2, authors, studies |
| Maps, local directories | branches |
| Registries, associations, SROs | home, About, licenses, specialists |
| Forums, Q&A | L3 problem pages, SKUs |
| Partners, dealers | company, cases, API, B2B pages |
| Tool citations | calculators, studies, glossaries, templates |

Per cluster: 2–3 different functional donor types. Passport per donor: topical rubric; cluster task it solves; real topical organic traffic; link naturalness. Rate by siteFocusScore/siteRadius, never DR/DA/total traffic alone. Prefer donors crediting a named author or company engineer. Guide/calculator links formatted as footnote citations to a primary source, not promo CTAs. Plan unlinked brand/expert/method mentions on authoritative sites for entity co-occurrence in AI Overviews.

Donor vetting checklist: media — topical focus, active readership; maps/directories — regional match, full NAP, direct branch URL; registries — official status, membership/certificate check; forums — live moderation, real pain discussion; partners — real operating ties; tool citations — link to the specific asset page.

Exclude: single donor type with uniform layouts; broad news portals or article dumps chosen for DR; disguised ads or unmoderated sponsor sections (Site Reputation Abuse); parasite placements in unrelated sponsored folders of universities or media; batch same-template exchanges (never 20–30 in one month).

## Guest-column vetting — 8 points (pp. 497–500)

1. Structure and editorial page exists.
2. Topical purity (last 30 posts).
3. Fresh content indexed within 1 month.
4. Traffic distribution (reject if ~99% lands on homepage or an old directory).
5. Outbound commercial density (every second article with 2–3 commercial shop links → link farm, block at any price).
6. Archive stability (sample 10 one-year-old articles).
7. rel attributes policy.
8. Main-rubric integration — article reachable by normal navigation, credited author and editor, not syndicated.

Sponsor folders (/promo/, /partner-post/) → no SEO value. Forced nofollow/sponsored → price for brand and referral only.

Pitch mechanics: ~7,000-char non-promotional column with checklists and anonymized charts; 3–4 follow-ups over 10–14 days, then 2–3 months rest; expected first-email reply 10–15%. Link in first half or middle of body inside a QBST passage — never in the author bio. Ask editors for 1–2 internal links from older indexed articles. Announce on publication day in your channels for real clicks. Alternative: extend a popular regularly updated donor article via a comment the author adds with a lasting link.

## Velocity guardrails (pp. 480, 486–487)

- Even weekly slots; never ~70% of volume on days 27–30.
- 2–4-week URL/branded burst after a study or calculator release.
- Local directories + city media at a branch opening.
- Gradual hub/L2 build as GSC impressions grow after an MVC launch.
- Buyer-guide placements 1.5–2 months before seasonal peaks.
- Site must be publishing/updating content in parallel — no new content for 6 months → do not start fast growth (~+30%/month).
- Vary article templates, link-paragraph position and anchor patterns within each sprint.
- Track link count and referring-domain count side by side: links up, domains flat → sitewide block or reused donor base.
- Never mix 5 strong links with 50 cheap directory/profile links in one wave.
- Drip-feeding manipulative links slowly does not make them compliant.
- Authoritative portals index in ~24 h.
