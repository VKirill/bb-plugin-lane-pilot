# Evidence-led on-page and draft quality gates

> [!IMPORTANT] Scope
> This is an independently worded engineering synthesis of the local book, not a reproduction. Source interpretations of Google internals are hypotheses, not exposed metrics or proof of current ranking behavior. Examples and numerical settings are not universal rules. Operational checks added by the compiler are identified below.

## Page contract

Require the target user task, approved page role, dated SERP observations, a source ledger and actual evidence assets. The central promise must agree across title, H1, important anchors and main content. Agreement is semantic; identical strings are unnecessary.

## Audit and production route

1. Compare declared title with observed SERP presentation. Locate mismatch, repetition, truncation or an unfulfilled promise. Preserve query/locale/device context when measuring CTR.
2. Build a coherent heading outline and standalone answer sections. Compare semantic tags with visual hierarchy and rendered interaction states. One H1 is an editorial convention in the source, not an established punishment rule.
3. Inventory claims, measurements, first-hand observations and comparisons. Tie each consequential claim to a real source or test record, including date, method and limitations. Send unsupported details to the data-gap list.
4. Replace repetitive generic sections with genuinely useful data, criteria, examples or decision support. Product variants, geographic names and paraphrases alone do not create distinct value. Do not fabricate experiments, mistakes or experience to appear human.
5. Validate images, tables, links and structured data against visible content. A review must separate own measurements from vendor specifications and disclose relevant commercial interests. Check the final page, not just the draft.

## QC artifact

Return a row per issue: location, violated promise, evidence, severity, proposed correction, missing input and acceptance check. Facts, schema and link destinations are hard gates; style is evaluated after those pass. Use an explicit hold when facts are insufficient instead of regenerating plausible claims.

## Measurement

For a material title or template change, use the controlled-experiment reference. Do not interpret the book's Goldmine/contentEffort attributes as accessible scores. An attractive title, a clean outline or a high critic rating is not proof of improved rankings.
