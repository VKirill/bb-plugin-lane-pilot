# Link and reputation evidence without causal overclaim

> [!IMPORTANT] Scope
> This is an independently worded engineering synthesis of the local book, not a reproduction. Source interpretations of Google internals are hypotheses, not exposed metrics or proof of current ranking behavior. Examples and numerical settings are not universal rules. Operational checks added by the compiler are identified below.

## Scope

Use for backlink, placement, brand and AI-reputation claims. Collect URL-level placements, target roles, context, dates, real referral evidence, dated branded SERPs and canonical brand facts. Separate observed exposure from conjecture about internal link tiers or user-validation systems.

## Diagnostic route

1. Verify placement status, destination, index/crawl access and editorial context. Check that the receiving page can answer the promised task before attributing failure to link weight.
2. Classify anchor families, repeated formulas, timing and donor topics. Investigate suspicious cohorts in context. A distribution or third-party score alone cannot establish a penalty or justify disavow.
3. Evaluate actual audience fit and the likelihood of useful discovery. Track real referrals and meaningful actions. Do not buy or manufacture clicks to simulate ranking signals.
4. For broken-link work, confirm actual unavailability, recover the original task from a real archive and verify that the replacement meets it. A model must not invent archival claims or sources.
5. Keep outreach as a reviewed draft until sending is authorized. Log placement, provenance and follow-up separately from any causal ranking verdict.

## Reputation observations

Test a fixed brand-question set against dated model outputs and search snapshots. Compare each material claim with authoritative company evidence. Correct first-party contradictions and unsupported public descriptions; do not present model answers as a view into Google's internal memory.

## Done when

Return observed facts, plausible mechanisms, counterevidence and the next check. Evaluate ranking hypotheses through a suitable control design. Referral value, brand recall and search rankings remain distinct outcomes; one cannot substitute for proof of another.
