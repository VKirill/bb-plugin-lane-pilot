## 1. Jobs to Be Done: Progress, Not Demographics (*Competing Against Luck*)

Demographics («мужчины 25–45, доход выше среднего») never cause a purchase. A customer **hires** a product in a specific struggling circumstance to make progress across three dimensions:
1. **Functional Job:** The practical task they need to complete.
2. **Emotional Job:** How they want to feel (or stop feeling — e.g., stop feeling anxious before tax deadlines or embarrassed in front of a client).
3. **Social Job:** How they want to be perceived by peers, bosses, or customers.

### Four High-Signal Clues During Discovery (Ch. 4–5)
- **Nonconsumption:** Where people currently do *nothing* because existing options are too expensive, complex, or intimidating.
- **Workarounds / Compensating Behaviors:** Duct-taped Google Sheets, Telegram saved messages, sticky notes, manual copy-pasting.
- **Things People Hate Doing:** Repetitive dread tasks where friction is tolerated only out of habit.
- **What Customers Fire:** Every time a customer *hires* your product, they *fire* something else. If you cannot name what gets fired, you do not yet understand the job.

## 2. The Four Forces of Progress (Switch Mechanics)

Every conversion on a landing page, onboarding screen, or sales call is governed by the balance of four forces:

$$\text{Switch happens iff } (\text{Push}_{\text{current pain}} + \text{Pull}_{\text{new solution}}) > (\text{Anxiety}_{\text{new choice}} + \text{Habit}_{\text{inertia}})$$

| Force | Passport Path | Discovery Question | How Downstream Agents Use It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Push (Толчок ситуации)** | `audience_jtbd.four_forces.push` | «Какое событие "последней капли" заставляет человека сказать "всё, так больше нельзя" и пойти искать решение?» | Hooks in Ads, Reels, Dzen, SEO problem-aware intros |
| **Pull (Притяжение нового)** | `audience_jtbd.four_forces.pull` | «Какой конкретный результат на другой стороне выглядит для него как облегчение и победа?» | Hero H1, value props, demo screenshots |
| **Anxiety (Тревога выбора)** | `audience_jtbd.four_forces.anxiety` | «Чего человек боится за секунду до оплаты или регистрации? ("потеряю данные", "сложно внедрять", "очередная пустышка", "не подойдёт под мою страну")» | `design-lead` & `mkt-landing-cro-audit` place micro-proofs, guarantees, and FAQ right next to CTA |
| **Habit (Инерция старого)** | `audience_jtbd.four_forces.habit` | «Что держит его в старом способе? ("в Excel уже всё настроено за 3 года", "команда привыкла")» | Zero-friction migration, 1-click import, free preview in product code (`writer`, `planner`) |

## 3. Adele Revella's 5 Rings of Buying Insight (High-Consideration Decisions)

Especially critical when buyer != end-user (B2B, clinics, agencies, expensive services) or when deals stall in «подумаем» (*fear of messing up*):

1. **Priority Initiative (`trigger_event`):** What changed to make solving this urgent *this week* rather than in six months?
2. **Success Factors:** Tangible business or personal outcomes expected from the purchase.
3. **Perceived Barriers:** Specific doubts or reasons buyers eliminate vendors (note: past mid-funnel, proving *they won't fail* beats promising bigger upside!).
4. **Decision Criteria:** Exact attributes buyers compare side-by-side (API, GDPR/SOC2, price transparency, speed, local support).
5. **Buyer's Journey (`payer_vs_user`):** Who uses it daily, who signs off the budget, who can veto the purchase, and what trusted sources they check first.
