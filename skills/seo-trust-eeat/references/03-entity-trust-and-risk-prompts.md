# Prompting for E-E-A-T, entity trust and claim risk

> [!NOTE] Provenance
> Independently worded engineering synthesis. Field names and acceptance contracts below are integration conventions, not verbatim source templates. Model outputs remain hypotheses until supported by observations.

## Intake

Collect the actual business identity, product/service scope, countries served, topic risks, available specialists, source documents, claims and current trust assets. Separate author, reviewer, editor and company roles. A profile, badge or schema property is not proof of credentials or work performed.

## Procedure

1. Classify where an incorrect statement could materially harm the reader and which claims require current primary evidence or domain review. Retain the jurisdiction and date as part of the claim.
2. Match the required proof to the page role and actual people available. Personal experience, technical reference and directive guidance have different evidentiary requirements.
3. Build a source-linked entity map connecting real organization, people, products and places. Only verified identity-equivalent profiles can support sameAs; do not invent affiliations or IDs.
4. Compare the trust requirements with existing assets and competitor observations. Missing data is a verification task, not permission to add plausible credentials, case studies or reviews.
5. Give the downstream brief a claim ledger: statement, source, scope, date, accountable reviewer, uncertainty and update trigger. High-risk unresolved claims hold the page.
