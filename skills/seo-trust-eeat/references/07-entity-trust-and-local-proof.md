# Entity trust, experience and local proof

> [!IMPORTANT] Scope
> This is an independently worded engineering synthesis of the local book, not a reproduction. Source interpretations of Google internals are hypotheses, not exposed metrics or proof of current ranking behavior. Examples and numerical settings are not universal rules. Operational checks added by the compiler are identified below.

## Trust contract

Collect real contributor identities, qualifications, editorial roles, business records, service conditions, original evidence and location data. Distinguish what the owner asserted from what has been independently checked. Topic risk determines the review and sourcing burden; an attractive biography is not proof of expertise.

## Procedure

1. Tie the author byline to a verified profile and relevant experience. Separate author, editor and specialist reviewer, and retain evidence that the claimed review actually occurred.
2. Connect organization, people and page markup to visible facts. Use sameAs only for genuine official or identity-equivalent profiles. Structured data must not invent affiliations, qualifications or ratings.
3. Build evidence footprints from actual measurements, cases, photos and disclosed limitations. Preserve attribution and dates. Do not invent personal failures or simulate lived experience.
4. For local content, reconcile real branches, addresses, phones, hours, service areas and GBP records. Distinguish actual local usefulness from city-name permutations. A virtual or fictional branch cannot be justified by query demand.
5. Review branded SERPs and genuine reviews for repeated service issues, identity confusion and unsupported claims. Route operational complaints to service owners and factual errors to source correction; do not manufacture positive reviews.

## Output and boundaries

Produce a trust-defect register with the unsupported statement, affected pages, evidence owner, verification method and review date. Internal attributes such as authorReputationScore, scamness or siteRadius are not available measurements. The book's numeric examples cannot become a trust score for the project. Completion means claims and roles are verifiable, not that a search engine has assigned a particular reputation value.
