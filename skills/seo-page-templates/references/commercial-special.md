# Selection, brand and comparison pages

## Commercial investigation funnel: curated selections, brand nodes, comparison pages (pp. 314-347)

**Trigger:** Capturing mid-funnel queries between categories and SKUs

**Inputs:** Cluster queries, Brand assortment and SKU counts, Attributes and use-case constraints

### Steps

1. Map cluster queries into five non-overlapping buckets: broad category, parametric filter, curated scenario, brand-in-category node, comparison.
2. Pick a chain: Category -> Selection -> SKU; Category -> Brand -> Comparison -> SKU; or, for large niches, Category -> Scenario -> Brand in scenario -> SKU.
3. Curated pages: a selection formula of 3-4 parameters, fits/does-not-fit conditions, sibling scenario presets and highlighted grid attributes.
4. Brand nodes: series navigator, proprietary technology explained, brand filters, comparison links.
5. Comparison pages: an HTML table of 6-10 criteria, conditional verdicts, TCO trade-offs, and outlinks split 60/20/20 across compared SKUs, brand nodes and selections. URL, H1 and title use the market model name.
6. Link each node from the parent category navigation and route visitors down to SKUs.

### Decision rules

- Few SKUs: drop the brand-in-scenario level (p.346).
- A compared model is discontinued: update to the current generation and keep or 301 the URL (p.347).

### Thresholds

- 3-4 parameters, 6-10 criteria, 60/20/20 (pp.346-347)

### Checklist

- Commercial bundle pre-launch checklist p.347

### Anti-patterns

- A global /brands/ directory instead of category brand nodes
- Comparison pages without SKU links

### Human checkpoints

- Editors write the conditional verdicts and trade-offs (p.346)
