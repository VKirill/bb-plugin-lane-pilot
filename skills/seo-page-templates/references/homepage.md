# Homepage

## Homepage: brand verification and link-weight routing (pp. 158-255)

**Trigger:** When building or auditing the homepage

**Inputs:** positioning and region, 10-15 priority level-2 hubs, trust facts, logos, licenses, cases, legal details and official profile URLs

### Steps

1. First screen: a clear H1, a subheading and 1-2 CTAs that convey specialization and region.
2. Navigation showcase: 1 click to 10-15 commercial hubs.
3. A compact showcase of flagship products or packages.
4. An E-E-A-T block with figures, logos, cases, licenses and short terms.
5. Remove long SEO text and hundreds of secondary links to tags, news or deep products.
6. Organization or LocalBusiness plus WebSite JSON-LD: legalName, logo, address, contactPoint, taxID, sameAs.

### Decision rules

- 2 or more failed criteria: rebuild the blocks and the outgoing links instead of adding keywords.

### Thresholds

- Clarity within 3-5 seconds
- Hubs: 10-15
- Depth: 1 click

### Checklist

- Positioning clear
- 1-click routing
- Trust signals with Organization schema
- No cannibalizing SEO text

### Anti-patterns

- Homepage of 10,000 characters with hundreds of links

### Human checkpoints

- A new visitor understands what the company does within 3-5 seconds

### Tools

- drmax-brandcore

### Feeds

- schema-jsonld-graph
- internal-linking-cocoon

**Output:** Focused homepage with root schema

**Done when:** All 4 criteria pass
