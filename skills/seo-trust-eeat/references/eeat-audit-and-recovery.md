# E-E-A-T audit, recovery, bootstrap, GBP, footprint — reference

Source: «SEO Монстр 2027» ch5 pp. 404–407, 415–417, 434–442; ch9 p. 718.

## E-E-A-T audit (pp. 404–407, 416)

Five gate questions every key section must answer with evidence:

1. Who is responsible?
2. Why are they competent in this narrow topic?
3. Which independent sources confirm it?
4. Any hidden conflicts or clutter?
5. Is acting on the page or transacting safe?

Six imitation-vs-real axes: expertise inside the text; author footprint; business proof; transparency; YMYL responsibility; post-click experience.

Reputation scan: brand, reviews, author names + specialty, searched with `-site:` exclusion across review sites, forums, maps, court dockets. Systemic complaints (non-performance, hidden charges, falsification) = critical threats. Track review tone and profile completeness over time.

Anti-patterns: unverifiable clichés ("20 years of experience", "approved by leading experts", "100% guarantee"); brand fame substituting for correctness.

## Trust recovery — five stages, strict order (pp. 415–417, 422–426)

1. **Main content**: remove errors/filler; add methodologies, tables, original photos, boundary conditions, Data-Shift.
2. **YMYL add-on**: 410 on templated regional doorway clones and near-duplicates; split broad pages by jurisdiction; purge outdated dates/limits/forms/law editions from body text; claims bound to Tier-1 sources.
3. **UI hygiene**: interstitials and noisy sitewide blocks removed; cart and forms fixed; snippets synced with first screen; bot-like traffic purged.
4. **Transparency**: legal details, refund/warranty, editorial policy, contacts published.
5. **External verification**: NAP standardized in GBP/directories; detailed independent reviews; real specialists publish in industry media.
6. **Packaging**: author/reviewer cards, badges, connected JSON-LD graph (Organization, Person, ProfilePage, Article).

Anti-patterns: doctor photo or random statute links on shallow text; fake date bumps.

## 90-day young-domain bootstrap (pp. 434–436)

Applies when hostAge <6–12 months, no behavioral/authority history.

- Entities first: Organization + author entities sameAs-linked to practitioners with 5–10 years external footprint.
- One narrow cocoon: 10–15 pages (1 Target + 3 Mixed + 8 Support); e-commerce starts with 1–2 niches.
- Vertical proof pack (see SKILL.md step 5.3 for the full per-vertical list).
- Own brand-verification queries with your own pages: reviews, legal details, pricing, documentation, cancellation, alternatives.
- GBP + vertical directories verified; 15–20 detailed external reviews; linked from /contacts/.

Forbidden: mass AI generation; fictional experts; bought reviews; YMYL advice by generalist copywriters; attacking 20 broad categories at once; internal-only review widgets.

## GBP operations (pp. 439–442)

Cadence:

- Weekly: phone line, open status, hours; all reviews and Q&A answered; 2–3 unretouched real-operation photos (p. 439).
- Monthly: categories, prices, showcase, attributes (payment, parking, accessibility); third-party/competitor edits.
- Quarterly: mystery-shopper stress test (build route, test call, website/booking links, UTM tags, load speed); review distribution across branches.
- Same day on disruption (repairs, moved entrance, closed parking, elevator, holidays): Google Posts + attributes.

Incident reply — six elements: acknowledge incident and visit date; responsible department/role; QC contact with ticket number; investigation deadline; public follow-up on action; invite rating revision.

Review routing: 2–4 h after CRM order closes (p. 442), personal link to that branch's review form and branch page; prompt mentions service, specialist, access. Never pool reviews company-wide; never ask for generic 5 stars.

Pickup retail: warehouse hours, vehicle access, loaders, returns + Merchant Center local inventory feed.

Vertical GBP fields: clinic, HoReCa, retail/pickup, auto service.

Anti-patterns: abandoned cards; logo-only photos; bot-template complaint replies.

## Entity footprint and brand poisoning (p. 718)

Footprint procedure (Entity Footprint Prompts 1–2, verbatim prompts in seo-prompt-kit):

1. Lock canonical identifiers: name, spelling variants, domain, markets, products, representatives, official profiles.
2. Prompt 1 on supplied/found materials only → confirmed attributes; conflicts with exact URLs and wording; missing data.
3. Company confirms the authoritative version of each conflicting fact — a discrepancy does not show which is right.
4. Prompt 2 → page edits, claim checks, profile syncs. New materials only where a basis exists.
5. Execute every action and verify on site and external profiles. A plan is not finished work.

Poisoning forensics (Brand Entity Poisoning Forensics v1):

1. Identifiers locked first — never collect mentions before this.
2. Mentions segmented by market, language, platform — namesake companies excluded.
3. Analyse recurring negative claims, conflicting descriptions, signs of coordinated publication.
4. Queue: pages to inspect; claims to check against company documents; legal/PR escalations.
5. Disputed or coordinated items → lawyer or communications specialist after a document check.
