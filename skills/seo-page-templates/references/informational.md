# Informational article

## Informational article build (pp. 172-264)

**Trigger:** When writing a new guide

**Inputs:** query subtype, primary sources (GOST/ISO, docs, field data), matching commercial pages

### Steps

1. Classify the query as factual, procedural, comparative or diagnostic, and choose modification A, B or C. Layouts are in main-content-assembly.
2. Answer in 2-3 sentences under the H1, then an anchor table of contents.
3. Modular H2-H3 sections with thesis summaries. Use tables and matrices instead of prose conditions.
4. A Zero-Click Defense artifact: a .docx or .xlsx template, a calculator or a copyable config.
5. Field experience with real photos, author and reviewer credits, a native commercial bridge.
6. Article or TechArticle JSON-LD with a Person author and multiple image ratios.

### Decision rules

- Short definitional topics never get the full 8-zone template.

### Thresholds

- Comparative table: 7-10 parameters
- See main-content-assembly

- Buy-now banners mid-article
- Mixing what-is, top-10 and setup in one article
