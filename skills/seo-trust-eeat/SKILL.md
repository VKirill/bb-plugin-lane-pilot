# SEO trust & E-E-A-T

Trust is built bottom-up: content correctness → UI hygiene → transparency → external verification → markup packaging. Schema on a shallow page is ignored; inflated claims trigger Lowest ratings. Source: «SEO Монстр 2027», ch5 pp. 404–446 and ch9 p. 718.

## When

- Building or fixing domain-level trust for e-commerce, YMYL, media, SaaS or local sites
- Creating or auditing author/reviewer attribution
- Trust or reputation audit (quarterly, or when trust is in question)
- Traffic drop on a low-trust or YMYL site
- Launching a domain <6–12 months old
- Any physical branch exists (GBP is ongoing)
- Reputation incident or conflicting brand data across markets
